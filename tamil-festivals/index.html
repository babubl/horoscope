Tamil festivals (pandigai) 2026–2027

Dates of every major Tamil festival in 2026–2027, calculated for Chennai, with what each festival is about.

2026

3Jan

Arudra Darisanam

Saturday · Margazhi 19

Arudra Darshanam in Margazhi: Nataraja’s cosmic dance, celebrated with kali at Chidambaram.

14Jan

Pongal / Makar Sankranti

Wednesday · Thai 1

The Tamil harvest festival on the first day of Thai: new rice is boiled with milk and jaggery until it overflows — "Pongalo Pongal!" — as thanks to the Sun.

25Jan

Ratha Saptami

Sunday · Thai 12

Ratha Saptami: the Sun turns his chariot north — the great festival of Suryanar Kovil.

1Feb

Thaipusam

Sunday · Thai 19

The full-moon day of Thai under Pusam star, sacred to Lord Murugan — devotees carry kavadi and milk pots to Palani and other Murugan temples.

15Feb

Maha Shivaratri

Sunday · Maasi 3

The great night of Shiva in Masi: fasting, night-long vigil and four kala poojas in every Shiva temple.

26Mar

Sri Rama Navami

Thursday · Panguni 12

Sri Rama Navami, the birth of Lord Rama.

1Apr

Panguni Uthiram

Wednesday · Panguni 18

Uthiram star in Panguni, the day of divine weddings — Shiva–Parvati, Murugan–Deivanai, Rama–Sita; great festivals at Murugan temples.

14Apr

Tamil New Year (Puthandu)

Tuesday · Chithirai 1

Tamil New Year, the first day of Chithirai, when the Sun enters Mesha. Homes are cleaned, kolams drawn and the new panchangam is read.

20Apr

Akshaya Tritiya

Monday · Chithirai 7

Akshaya Tritiya: whatever good is begun today is believed to grow without end.

1May

Chithra Pournami

Friday · Chithirai 18

The full moon of Chithirai, sacred to Chitragupta; lakhs walk the Girivalam at Tiruvannamalai.

30May

Vaikasi Visakam

Saturday · Vaikasi 16

Visakam star in Vaikasi — the birthday of Lord Murugan.

29Jul

Guru Purnima

Wednesday · Aadi 13

Guru Pournami: honouring one’s teachers and Vyasa.

3Aug

Aadi Perukku

Monday · Aadi 18

18th of Aadi: thanksgiving to the Kaveri and all rivers as they swell with the monsoon; women offer prayers at the river banks.

12Aug

Aadi Amavasai

Wednesday · Aadi 27

The new moon of Aadi, one of the most important days for tarpanam to ancestors — crowds gather at Rameswaram and river banks.

21Aug

Varalakshmi Vratam

Friday · Aavani 5

Married women worship Goddess Varalakshmi for the family’s prosperity, on the Friday before the Aavani full moon.

28Aug

Raksha Bandhan / Avani Avittam

Friday · Aavani 12

Aavani Avittam: the day of changing the sacred thread (upakarma).

4Sep

Krishna Janmashtami

Friday · Aavani 19

Gokulashtami, the birth of Lord Krishna — little footprints are drawn from the doorstep to the pooja room.

14Sep

Ganesh Chaturthi

Monday · Aavani 29

Vinayaka Chathurthi: clay Ganesha idols, kozhukattai and modakam.

10Oct

Mahalaya Amavasya

Saturday · Purattasi 24

Mahalaya Amavasai closes the fortnight of the ancestors (Mahalaya Paksham) — the most important tarpanam day of the year.

11Oct

Navaratri begins

Sunday · Purattasi 25

Nine nights of the Goddess begin; homes set up the golu (kolu) of dolls.

19Oct

Saraswati / Ayudha Puja

Monday · Aippasi 2

Saraswati Pooja and Ayudha Pooja: books, instruments, tools and vehicles are worshipped.

20Oct

Vijayadashami / Dussehra

Tuesday · Aippasi 3

Vijayadasami: victory of good; children begin their learning (Vidyarambham).

8Nov

Deepavali (Naraka Chaturdashi)

Sunday · Aippasi 22

Deepavali in Tamil Nadu is celebrated at dawn on Naraka Chaturdasi — oil bath, new clothes, sweets and crackers.

8Nov

Diwali – Lakshmi Puja

Sunday · Aippasi 22

Lakshmi Pooja on the Deepavali new moon.

15Nov

Skanda Shashti (Soorasamharam)

Sunday · Aippasi 29

Kanda Sashti: six days of fasting ending with Soorasamharam, Murugan’s victory over Surapadman — grandest at Thiruchendur.

24Nov

Karthigai Deepam

Tuesday · Karthigai 8

Thirukarthigai: rows of lamps in every home; the Maha Deepam is lit atop the Tiruvannamalai hill.

20Dec

Vaikunta Ekadashi

Sunday · Margazhi 5

Vaikunta Ekadasi: the Paramapada Vasal (gate of heaven) opens in Vishnu temples — Srirangam above all.

24Dec

Arudra Darisanam

Thursday · Margazhi 9

Arudra Darshanam in Margazhi: Nataraja’s cosmic dance, celebrated with kali at Chidambaram.
