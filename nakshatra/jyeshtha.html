Jyeshtha Nakshatra: marriage matching

Jyeshtha is nakshatra number 18 of 27, ruled by Mercury. People born in Jyeshtha belong to the Rakshasa gana, with Deer yoni, Aadi nadi and Pada (feet) rajju.
