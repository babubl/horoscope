Shatabhisha Nakshatra: marriage matching

Shatabhisha is nakshatra number 24 of 27, ruled by Rahu. People born in Shatabhisha belong to the Rakshasa gana, with Horse yoni, Aadi nadi and Kanta (neck) rajju.
