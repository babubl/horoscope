Purva Ashadha Nakshatra: marriage matching

Purva Ashadha is nakshatra number 20 of 27, ruled by Venus. People born in Purva Ashadha belong to the Manushya gana, with Monkey yoni, Madhya nadi and Kati (waist) rajju.
