Thirumanancheri Kalyanasundareswarar Temple

Thirumanancheri is where, by tradition, Lord Shiva married Parvati, and it is the best-known temple in Tamil Nadu for prayers for marriage. Devotees receive a garland, keep it at home, and return with it as a couple after the wedding.

Sthala varalaru — the temple’s story

Parvati is said to have done penance here to win Shiva, and their wedding took place at this village — "Thiru-manam-cheri", the place of the sacred wedding. Another local story says Manmatha, burnt by Shiva’s third eye, was restored to life here at his wife Rati’s plea.

As told in temple tradition.

History and architecture

A Paadal Petra sthalam on the north bank of the Kaveri, sung by Sambandar and Appar, and described as a 10th-century Chola temple. It is called a "nitya kalyana kshetram", where a divine wedding ritual is said to take place every day.

Parigaram done here

  • Bow at the flagstaff, worship Selva Ganapathi and light five ghee lamps at the deepa mandapam.
  • Attend the marriage-prayer archanai and receive a garland in a small bag; keep it in the home pooja room.
  • After the wedding, the couple returns with the garland, offers it back and gives thanks.
  • Mangala Rahu here is also worshipped, especially on Amavasai.

How to reach

About 6 km from Kuthalam, 8 km from Mayiladuthurai and 32 km from Kumbakonam. Nearest railway stations: Kuthalam and Mayiladuthurai Junction.

Before you go

  • Weekends and auspicious days are very crowded — arrive early.
  • Keep the garland safely; you bring it back after the wedding.
