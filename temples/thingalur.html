Thingalur Kailasanathar Temple

Thingalur — "the village of the Moon" — is the Navagraha temple for Chandran. It is a quiet Chola-era Shiva temple near Thiruvaiyaru, also known as the birthplace of the Nayanar saint Appoothi Adigal.

Sthala varalaru — the temple’s story

Appoothi Adigal, a devoted follower of Appar (Thirunavukkarasar), arranged a feast for the saint. His son died of a snakebite while cutting plantain leaves for it, and Appar is said to have brought the boy back to life by singing a Thevaram hymn. Sekkizhar tells the story in the Periya Puranam.

As told in temple tradition.

History and architecture

The temple is attributed to the Cholas, with expansions recorded under later Chola kings; the present stone structure is largely from the Nayak period (16th century). Appar mentions it in the Thevaram, making it a Thevara Vaippu sthalam.

Chandran has his own shrine in the first prakaram, facing the main deity; the black granite image is usually dressed in white.

Parigaram done here

  • White cloth and white flowers for Chandran; raw rice and paddy as offerings.
  • Milk payasam or curd rice as naivedyam; worship on Mondays and full-moon days.
  • Many families bring their babies here for annaprasanam (first rice feeding), traditionally after showing the child the moon.

How to reach

About 4 km from Thiruvaiyaru on the Thiruvaiyaru–Kumbakonam road; about 35 km from Kumbakonam and 18 km from Thanjavur, the nearest railway junction. Buses run from Thanjavur, Thiruvaiyaru and Kumbakonam.

Before you go

  • The east tower faces fields, so enter from the south side.
  • It is the westernmost of the nine; combine it with Thiruvaiyaru or Thanjavur.
