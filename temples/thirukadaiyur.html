Thirukadaiyur Abirami – Amirthaghateswarar Temple

Thirukadaiyur is where, by tradition, Lord Shiva defeated Yama to save the young Markandeya. It is the most sought-after temple in Tamil Nadu for Shashtiabdapoorthi (60th birthday), Bheemaratha Shanti (70th) and Sathabhishekam (80th) ceremonies.

Sthala varalaru — the temple’s story

Markandeya was destined to die at sixteen. When Yama came for him, the boy clung to the lingam, and Shiva emerged as Kalasamharamurthy to defeat Yama. The name comes from the pot (ghatam) of amrita kept here. The poet Abirami Bhattar is said to have been saved when the goddess made a full moon shine on a new-moon night; he then sang the Abirami Anthathi.

As told in temple tradition.

History and architecture

The temple is a Paadal Petra sthalam sung by Appar, Sundarar and Sambandar, and one of the eight Ashta Veerattanam temples that mark Shiva’s heroic deeds. Inscriptions go back to Rajaraja I; Kulottunga Chola I rebuilt the brick walls in stone, and the Thanjavur Nayaks enlarged it. It is administered by the Dharmapuram Adheenam.

Parigaram done here

  • Couples perform Ugraratha Shanti (60), Shashtiabdapoorthi (61st year), Bheemaratha Shanti (70) and Sathabhishekam (80) here.
  • Ayush homam and Mrityunjaya homam for health and long life.
  • Reciting the Abirami Anthathi is a cherished devotion here.

How to reach

About 21 km east of Mayiladuthurai, 15 km north of Karaikal and about 50 km from Kumbakonam. Nearest railway station: Mayiladuthurai Junction (about 23 km).

Before you go

  • Book the homam with the temple office well ahead, especially on auspicious muhurtham days.
  • Confirm package prices directly with the temple, not through agents.
