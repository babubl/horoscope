Temple guide: Navagraha and parigaram temples of Tamil Nadu

The nine Navagraha temples around Kumbakonam, the Thanjavur Big Temple and the temples families visit for marriage, children, long life and ancestral rites — with the deity, history, the parigaram done there, darshan timings and how to reach.

The nine Navagraha temples

All nine lie within about 60 km of Kumbakonam, in the Kaveri delta. Most of them are ancient Shiva temples where the planet is worshipped in its own shrine; Suryanar Kovil is the only one built for the Sun himself.

Planning the Navagraha tour

Stay in Kumbakonam. With a car, all nine temples can be covered in one long day or comfortably in two days.

Day 1 — south and west of Kumbakonam

  1. Suryanar Kovil — Sun (Suriyan)
  2. Kanjanur Agniswarar Temple — Venus (Sukran)
  3. Thirunageswaram Naganathaswamy Temple — Rahu
  4. Alangudi Apatsahayeswarar Temple — Jupiter (Guru / Viyazhan)
  5. Thingalur Kailasanathar Temple — Moon (Chandran)

Day 2 — towards the coast

  1. Vaitheeswaran Koil — Mars (Sevvai / Angarakan)
  2. Thiruvenkadu Swetharanyeswarar Temple — Mercury (Budhan)
  3. Keezhaperumpallam Naganathaswamy Temple — Ketu
  4. Thirunallar Saneeswaran Temple (Dharbaranyeswarar) — Saturn (Sani)

Tradition prefers starting with Suryanar Kovil (the Sun) and ending at Thirunallar (Saturn). Temples close at midday, roughly 12:00 or 12:30 to 4:00 pm, so plan the morning and evening rounds around that.
