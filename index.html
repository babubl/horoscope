Is Suba Jathagam really free?

Yes. Charts, matching, panchangam and the printable horoscope are free, with no sign-up.

Which matching method should I use?

Tamil and Kerala families usually follow the 10 porutham (with Rajju and Vedhai); Telugu, Kannada and North Indian families usually follow the 36-guna Ashtakoota (Guna Milan). Suba Jathagam shows both, and your tradition decides which comes first.

How accurate are the calculations?

Planet positions come from a modern astronomical engine with the Lahiri ayanamsa. Tithi, nakshatra, Rahu kalam and the Tamil, Malayalam and lunar calendar dates are worked out from the actual sunrise at the birth place.

Can I print a traditional horoscope?

Yes — a two-page horoscope in your culture’s style: manjal-kungumam for Tamil, kasavu gold for Kerala, a mango-leaf thoranam for Telugu, kumkum and green for Kannada, and saffron with swastik and kalash for North India. Print it or save it as a PDF.

Is my birth data stored?

No. Everything is calculated inside your browser; nothing is sent to our server.
