Parigaram: traditional remedies

What Tamil tradition suggests for each of the nine planets and for Chevvai dosham, Rahu–Ketu dosham, Ezharai Sani, delays in marriage, children, long life and ancestral rites — with the temple for each.

Parigaram is a matter of faith. It is not a substitute for medical, legal or financial help. Simple prayer, a lamp and charity are the heart of every remedy; be careful of anyone who demands large payments or frightens you with a dosha.

Chevvai (Mars) dosham

Mars in the 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house from the Lagna or Moon is called Chevvai dosham. Tradition first checks whether it is cancelled (Mars in its own or exalted sign, aspects of Jupiter and similar rules) — our free horoscope shows this. The most important traditional remedy is matching: a partner with a similar dosha balances it (dosha samyam).

  • Worship Lord Murugan on Tuesdays; read or listen to Kanda Sashti Kavasam.
  • Observe the Sashti fast (sixth day of the waxing moon), especially Kanda Sashti.
  • Visit Vaitheeswaran Koil, the Angaraka (Mars) sthalam, and offer red cloth and toor dal.
  • Light a lamp with red wicks on Tuesdays; donate toor dal or red cloth to those in need.
  • Before marriage, match horoscopes carefully — dosha samyam matters more than any ritual.

Temple: Vaitheeswaran Koil

Rahu–Ketu (Naga / Sarpa) dosham

Rahu or Ketu in the 1st, 2nd, 7th or 8th house is traditionally called Rahu–Ketu or Naga dosham and is considered mainly for marriage. As with Chevvai dosham, a partner with a similar placement balances it.

  • Visit Thirunageswaram (Rahu) and Keezhaperumpallam (Ketu), usually together on the same day.
  • Worship Goddess Durga during Rahu kalam — many families light a lamp in a halved lemon on Tuesdays, Fridays or Sundays.
  • Pray to Lord Vinayaka for Ketu; offer arugu (durva grass).
  • Offer milk to the Naga stones under a peepal or neem tree, especially on Naga Panchami.
  • Donate black gram (for Rahu) or horse gram (for Ketu).

Temple: Thirunageswaram Naganathaswamy Temple · Keezhaperumpallam Naganathaswamy Temple

Kala Sarpa yoga

When all seven planets fall on one side of the Rahu–Ketu axis, the chart is said to have Kala Sarpa yoga. Many astrologers treat it as a yoga to understand rather than a dosha to fear; its effect depends on the rest of the chart.

  • Rahu–Ketu worship at Thirunageswaram and Keezhaperumpallam.
  • Srikalahasti in Andhra Pradesh, about 115 km from Chennai, is the best-known temple for Rahu–Ketu and Kala Sarpa pooja.
  • Naga Panchami and Naga Chaturthi worship at home or at a Naga shrine.

Temple: Thirunageswaram Naganathaswamy Temple · Keezhaperumpallam Naganathaswamy Temple

Ezharai Sani, Ashtama Sani and Kandaka Sani

Saturn’s transit through the 12th, 1st and 2nd houses from your Moon sign is Ezharai (seven-and-a-half year) Sani; the 8th is Ashtama Sani and the 4th, 7th and 10th are Kandaka Sani. Our horoscope shows your current Saturn phase. Tradition teaches patience, discipline and service during these years.

  • Light a sesame-oil (ellu) lamp at the Sani shrine on Saturdays.
  • Visit Thirunallar, the Saturn sthalam near Karaikal; devotees bathe in the Nala theertham before darshan.
  • Worship Hanuman or Sastha (Ayyappan) on Saturdays; Shiva worship on Pradosham.
  • Feed crows (Saturn’s vahanam) and help the elderly, workers and people with disabilities.
  • Donate black sesame or a dark cloth; avoid starting major ventures in haste.

Temple: Thirunallar Saneeswaran Temple (Dharbaranyeswarar)

Guru (Jupiter) — transit and Guru balam

Jupiter in the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th or 11th house from the Moon is considered favourable (Guru balam), which families look for before a marriage. When Jupiter’s transit is unfavourable, tradition turns to Guru worship.

  • Worship Dakshinamurthy on Thursdays with yellow flowers and a garland of Bengal gram (kondai kadalai).
  • Visit Alangudi, the Guru sthalam near Kumbakonam, especially on Guru peyarchi (Jupiter transit) day.
  • Respect and serve teachers and elders; donate yellow cloth, Bengal gram or books.

Temple: Alangudi Apatsahayeswarar Temple

Delay in marriage

Families usually check Chevvai and Rahu–Ketu dosham, the 7th house and Guru balam. Alongside a careful match, these are the commonly followed prayers.

  • Thirumanancheri near Kuthalam is the best-known temple in Tamil Nadu for prayers for marriage; devotees return together after the wedding to give thanks.
  • Worship Goddess Parvati / Andal; many young women recite Andal’s Thiruppavai in the month of Margazhi.
  • Thursday Guru worship when Jupiter’s transit is weak; Tuesday Murugan worship for Chevvai dosham.

Temple: Thirumanancheri Kalyanasundareswarar Temple

Prayers for children and a safe pregnancy

Couples hoping for a child, and expectant mothers, traditionally pray to Garbharakshambigai — the Goddess who protects the womb. Prayer goes hand in hand with good medical care, never in place of it.

  • Visit Thirukarukavur near Papanasam (Thanjavur district) and receive the blessed ghee or castor oil prasadam given there.
  • Worship on Fridays and Pournami (full moon); recite the Garbharakshambigai sloka.
  • Keep regular check-ups with your doctor — tradition and medicine work together.

Temple: Thirukarukavur Garbharakshambigai Temple

Long life — 60th, 70th and 80th birthdays

Shashtiabdapoorthi (60 years), Bheemaratha Shanti (70) and Sathabhishekam (80) are celebrated as thanksgiving for a long life and to pray for health in the years ahead.

  • Thirukadaiyur, where tradition says Lord Shiva saved young Markandeya from Yama, is the most sought-after temple for these ceremonies; book well in advance.
  • Ayush homam on the birth nakshatra; Mrityunjaya mantra japam.

Temple: Thirukadaiyur Abirami – Amirthaghateswarar Temple

Pitru (ancestral) rites

Offering tarpanam (water and sesame) to departed ancestors is a duty in Hindu tradition, and many families believe neglecting it brings obstacles.

  • Tarpanam on every Amavasai, and especially on Thai Amavasai, Aadi Amavasai and Mahalaya Amavasai — see our festival calendar for the dates.
  • Rameswaram: bathe in the sea at Agni Theertham, then in the 22 theerthams inside the Ramanathaswamy temple, followed by darshan.
  • Feed the needy and crows on the ancestors’ tithi (death anniversary).

Temple: Rameswaram Ramanathaswamy Temple

During a difficult dasa or bhukti

Our horoscope shows the current mahadasa and bhukti. When a period is difficult, tradition suggests worshipping the planet that rules it.

  • Pray on that planet’s weekday and visit its Navagraha temple (see the table above).
  • Recite its mantra 108 times; donate its grain on its weekday.
  • At any Shiva temple, walk around the Navagraha shrine nine times calmly — never touch the idols.
  • Wear a gemstone only after an experienced astrologer has checked the full chart — a wrong stone is thought to do more harm than good.
